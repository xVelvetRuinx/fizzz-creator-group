WHAT I LOOK FOR

I’m not looking for follower counts. I’m looking for craft.

Established does not mean famous. It means you can show that you understand what makes an RP character compelling, playable, and worth returning to.

01

Character depth

A distinct person with motives, contradictions, boundaries, and room for the user to matter.

02

Strong openings

A scene that starts moving immediately, establishes the dynamic, and gives the user something to answer.

03

Playable RP

Characters that create possibilities instead of trapping the user inside a predetermined story.

04

Presentation

A coherent concept, clean character card, strong visual identity, and enough polish to earn the click.

FAQ

Questions before you reach out?

If your situation is unusual, DM me. I would rather look at your work than make you guess whether you fit.

Do I need a large following?

No. I care much more about the quality and playability of your characters than your follower count.

Can I already be creating on another platform?

Yes. The established route is specifically useful for creators who already have characters or a portfolio elsewhere and want to bring strong existing work to Fizzz.

Is paid work guaranteed?

No. Trial participation, migration, or joining my group does not guarantee a contract or specific payment. Fizzz evaluates qualifying work and official written terms control.

What should I send you first?

Your creator profile or portfolio, links or screenshots for 1–3 of your strongest characters, the platforms you currently create on, and a short note about the kind of RP you like to build.

What if I’m talented but new to character bots?

Use the New & Growing route. Strong fiction writers, roleplayers, visual creators, and interactive-storytelling people can have excellent instincts even if the card format is new to them.

Who handles contracts and payment?

Fizzz handles official evaluation, reward confirmation, contracts, settlement, and payment. My role is creator discovery, pre-screening, onboarding support, training, and group guidance.
